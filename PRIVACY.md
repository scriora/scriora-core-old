# Privacy notice

**Product:** Scriora  
**Contact:** mohamedshaban_@outlook.com  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-10

This notice is for people who connect an official social account (starting with LinkedIn) to Scriora, and for operators who self-host the software.

## What we collect

When you authorize LinkedIn (or another official provider):

- your workspace identity in Scriora
- the provider account id, name, and granted scopes
- access and refresh tokens (encrypted at rest; never shown in the browser)
- content you choose to draft, schedule, or publish
- publication attempt records needed to avoid duplicate posts

We do not receive your LinkedIn password. Authorization uses LinkedIn’s OAuth screen.

## How we use it

- connect your account
- publish or schedule only after your (or your workspace’s) approval
- show capabilities that were actually granted; unsupported features stay off
- operate and secure the service

We do **not** sell your data. We do **not** use your private posts or metrics to train public models.

## Storage and self-hosting

Open-source Scriora can run on infrastructure you control. Then this data stays on your servers, subject to your own policies.

A later managed cloud, if you use it, stores the same classes of data for your tenant only.

## Sharing

We send tokens and publish requests only to the official provider APIs you connected. We do not share customer content with unrelated third parties except as required by law or to operate subprocessors you are told about.

## Retention and deletion

Disconnecting an account deletes local token ciphertext where the product supports disconnect. You may ask for deletion of tenant data at the contact above. Provider copies of posts you published remain under that provider’s rules.

## Changes

We will update this file when the product’s data practices change.
